# Aspire vs Production Observability

Aspire is especially valuable for local development, orchestration, service discovery, and telemetry integration.

For production, think in layers:
- application hosting/deployment,
- Orleans clustering and storage,
- metrics/logs/traces,
- dashboards and alerting,
- resilience and scaling.

Do not treat the Aspire development dashboard as the entire production monitoring strategy.
